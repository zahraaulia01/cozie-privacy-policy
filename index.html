Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Cozie is a wardrobe management and outfit planning application designed to help users organize clothing collections, create outfits, and plan daily fashion activities.

Information We Collect

  • Email address for account authentication.
  • Clothing images uploaded by users.
  • Wardrobe information.
  • Outfit collections.
  • Planner and scheduling information.
  • Favorites and user preferences.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide wardrobe management features.
  • Save your outfits and planner data.
  • Synchronize your account across devices.
  • Improve application performance.
  • Maintain account security.

Data Storage

User data is stored securely using Supabase cloud services.

Data Sharing

Cozie does not sell or rent users' personal information. Data is only used to provide application functionality.

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information.

Children's Privacy

Cozie is not intended for children under the age of 13.
